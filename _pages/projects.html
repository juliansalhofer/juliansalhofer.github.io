  • Visualizing Aging Processes of Oil Paintings on Canvas

    Developing an interactive VR framework to simulate environmental degradation of oil paintings, supporting conservation decision-making and empirical perception research.

    Jan 2026 – Dec 2028 · funded by GFF

  • OrDiV – Art Experience in the (Post-)Digital Age

    Investigating how the perception of original artworks differs from digital and virtual reproductions, in cooperation with Universität für Weiterbildung Krems and the Belvedere Research Center. Contribution includes high-fidelity photogrammetric reconstruction of exhibition spaces and VR integration for comparative user studies.

    Mar 2024 – Mar 2027 · funded by FWF

  • VRinMotion

    Exploring how the characteristics of analogue stop-motion animation can be adapted and re-imagined within interactive VR environments, across four ExperiMotion experiments in collaboration with internationally established animation artists.

    Jan 2022 – Jun 2025 · funded by FWF

  • VeRgonomiX

    Motion capture data clean-up and editing to support ergonomics research in virtual environments.

    Sep 2020 – Feb 2024 · funded by AUVA
