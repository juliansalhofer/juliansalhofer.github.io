I am a PhD student at the [Research Unit of Computer Graphics](https://www.cg.tuwien.ac.at/), [TU Wien](https://www.tuwien.ac.at/), and a university assistant (*prae-doc*) jointly at the [University of Applied Sciences St. Pölten (USTP)](https://research.ustp.at/en) and the [University for Continuing Education Krems (UWK)](https://www.donau-uni.ac.at/en.html).

My work sits at the intersection of **computer graphics**, **virtual reality**, and **cultural heritage** -- combining technical development with artistic and empirical research.

## Research

My PhD thesis, *[Visualizing Aging Processes of Oil Paintings on Canvas: An Interactive VR Approach](https://research.ustp.at/en/projects/visualizing-aging-processes-of-oil-paintings-on-canvas-an-interactive-vr-approach)*, develops a VR-based framework for simulating and interactively visualizing how environmental factors (such as temperature, humidity, UV radiation) change oil paintings over time. The framework integrates procedural texturing, Physically Based Rendering (PBR), and GPU-accelerated algorithms to support art conservators and curators, and to enable empirical user studies on aesthetic perception of aged artworks. The project is funded by the GFF (FTI-Dissertations 2024), and runs from January 2026 to December 2028.

I am supervised by [Univ.Prof. Dipl.Ing. Dr. Eduard Gröller](https://www.cg.tuwien.ac.at/staff/EduardGroeller) (TU Wien) and [FH-Prof. Dr. Hsiang-Yun Wu](https://www.donau-uni.ac.at/en/university/organization/employees/person/4295324511) (TU Wien & USTP) and [Dr. Hanna Brinkmann](https://www.donau-uni.ac.at/en/university/organization/employees/person/4295324511) (UWK) .

Beyond my PhD, my broader research interests include:

- Virtual and mixed reality environments
- Immersive media and expanded animation
- 3D reconstruction for cultural heritage
- Spatial audio for interactive animated environments

## Industry Experience

In parallel with my research career, I have worked as a **Technical 3D Animator / Animation Fixer** at [arx anima animation studio](https://www.arxanima.com/) in Vienna (Apr--Sep 2025), contributing to the CG animated feature film [*Monster Mia*](https://www.imdb.com/de/title/tt38807889/) (theatrical release Oct. 2026). My work included dynamics simulation, animation fixes, AnimCheck QA, and custom PyMel scripting.
